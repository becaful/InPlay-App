# inPlay | Golf Statistics
*Your game, in perspective.*

## Overview

InPlay is a native Android golf performance tracking application designed to make golf statistics more accessible, relevant and actionable for everyday golfers, with a particular focus on women and mid-to-high-handicap players.

Originally designed and developed as part of my Bachelor of Information Sciences at Massey University in 2025, InPlay brings together my interests in golf, digital product development, user experience and data-driven decision-making.

## Product Vision

This opportunity was identified through my own experience using established golf performance tracking ecosystems such as Garmin. The inclusion of mishits in club distance statistics, alongside an emphasis on comparative metrics such as strokes gained, often produced insights that were not representative of my playing ability or aligned with my improvement needs. This highlighted an opportunity to better contextualise performance data and translate it into relevant, actionable insights for recreational golfers.

Many existing golf performance tracking solutions emphasise distance, advanced analytics and comparison against highly skilled players. Whilst valuable to some golfers, these metrics do not necessarily reflect the priorities or improvement opportunities of the everyday recreational golfer.

InPlay aims to take a more individualised approach to performance, focusing on:

- Personal performance rather than external benchmarks.
- Scoring efficiency and consistency.
- Identifying patterns and opportunities for improvement.
- Supporting informed on-course decision-making.
- Making performance data accessible without overwhelming the user.
- Contextualising established performance benchmarks to support individual game improvement strategies.

The underlying philosophy is that improving your golf game is not solely about improving your technical ability, but also about better understanding and managing the game you already play.

## Target Audience

InPlay is designed primarily for recreational golfers, particularly women and mid-to-high-handicap players who want to better understand their performance.

The product concept is informed by my own experience as an amateur golfer and through my involvement in the organisation and management of a women's golf society in Abu Dhabi.

## MVP Development

The initial application was developed within the scope and time constraints of an academic assessment. Development prioritised establishing the core functionality and data architecture necessary to support the broader product vision. The MVP focused on structured round and granular stroke-level data collection, local data persistence and establishing a foundation for future performance analytics.

This approach balanced academic assessment requirements and development constraints with the long-term product vision, prioritising the underlying data architecture and core user journey, to enable additional features and analytical capabilities to be introduced incrementally.

## Future Product Direction

Potential future development includes:

- Personalised performance insights.
- Club performance and distance analysis.
- Identification of avoidable scoring losses (e.g. leaked strokes due to mishits and 3 putts).
- Course management insights.
- Longitudinal performance trends.
- Data-informed practice recommendations.
- Expanded course and tee-box support.
- Handicap benchmarking and prediction.
- Contextualised improvement targets and benchmarks based on handicap bands (e.g. adapting traditional metrics such as up-and-down and greens-in-regulation to account for handicap-adjusted scoring expectations).

The long-term objective is to move beyond descriptive statistics towards meaningful, actionable insights that help golfers improve their individual game.

## Project Status

This repository contains a public portfolio version of my original academic project. It represents an early-stage product implementation rather than a commercially released application.

The project was undertaken independently, with end-to-end responsibility for product conceptualisation, requirements definition, MVP scoping, branding, user experience, interface design, database architecture and application development.
